# LIME IME Backlog

Public backlog for confirmed unresolved fixes, source-fixed follow-up, and approved product work. Issue-specific investigation details stay in `docs/#NN_ISSUE.md`; fully resolved scopes stay in their issue docs instead of here.

Version-line policy: `6.0.x` and `6.1.x` are bug-fix-only. All new app product work (`feat#...`) targets `6.2.x`, must cover both Android and iOS, and must not be backported to `6.0.x` or `6.1.x`. Platform-specific implementation and release verification remain separate gates.
